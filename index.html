Voxel Heroes by Tront

Voxel Heroes is a free action adventure in the browser, in the spirit of 3D Dot Game Heroes and the SNES Zelda overworld. Explore a little block world screen by screen: cut grass, smash pots, push statues, read the signs, and drop into a dungeon with a serpent waiting at the bottom.

The hero fights with a sword that charges into a spin, a boomerang, bombs and a shield, and the town inventor hands over dash boots. Mossbrook runs on a day clock: the townsfolk keep their own schedules, chat with each other, take one gift a day and warm up to you the more you visit.

Built with Three.js, with the hero, the townsfolk and the foes modelled in Boxel, Tront's own per-face voxel art tool. Made by Tront (Trent Sterling) with AI coding tools, as one of a run of small browser games shipped in public.
